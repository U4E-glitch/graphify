## Full description (EN)

```
Qiyas is a private, fully offline diary for your blood glucose and blood pressure.

YOUR DATA NEVER LEAVES YOUR PHONE
Qiyas has no internet access — the network permission simply doesn't exist in the app. No account, no cloud, no ads, no analytics. Your health data is yours.

LOG IN SECONDS
• Glucose in mg/dL or mmol/L, with the measurement context (fasting, before/after meals, bedtime, random)
• Blood pressure with optional pulse (morning/evening)
• Snap a photo of your meter — Qiyas reads the numbers on-device and pre-fills them for you to confirm
• Every reading is classified instantly with clear color zones, and dangerous values show an immediate safety alert

UNDERSTAND YOUR WEEK
• Dashboard with your latest readings and 7-day trends
• Weekly report: averages, % in range, highs & lows, trends vs last week
• Practical lifestyle notes — never medication advice
• Export the report as PDF or all data as CSV to share with your physician

MADE FOR ENGLISH & ARABIC
Full right-to-left Arabic interface with clear, consistent numerals.

Qiyas is a personal tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The ranges shown are general adult guidelines and may not match your personal targets. Always follow your physician's guidance.
```
